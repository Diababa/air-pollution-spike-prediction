# Baseline comparison

Generated by `python -m src.models.baseline`. Every metric below is computed on the **same** test set.

## Evaluation protocol

- **Chronological** 80 / 20 split. No shuffling, no random cross-validation: both would leak future information into training.
- Train: 20,870 hours (2023-05-04 to 2025-09-19).
- Test: 5,218 hours (2025-09-19 to 2026-04-24), strictly after the training window.
- Spike threshold: 90th percentile of PM2.5 = 20.3 ug/m3. Spikes are 12.2 % of the test set.
