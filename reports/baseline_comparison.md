# Baseline comparison

Generated by `python -m src.models.baseline`. Every metric below is computed on the **same** test set.

## Evaluation protocol

- **Chronological** 80 / 20 split. No shuffling, no random cross-validation: both would leak future information into training.
- Train: 20,851 hours (2023-10-19 to 2026-03-05).
- Test: 5,213 hours (2026-03-05 to 2026-10-08), strictly after the training window.
- Spike threshold: 90th percentile of PM2.5 = 20.1 ug/m3. Spikes are 4.3 % of the test set.
